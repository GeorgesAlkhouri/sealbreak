## Contributor License Agreement

Before merging a copyright-bearing contribution, contributors must accept the Harmony Contributor License Agreement (CLA) with **Outbound License Option Five**. For individuals, the GitHub CLA Assistant records this acceptance directly in the pull request. Contributors keep copyright ownership, while granting the maintainer the additional rights needed for commercial and proprietary licensing.

The agreement is the [Individual Contributor License Agreement](docs/legal/CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md). A GitHub CLA Assistant requests electronic acceptance directly in the pull request. Read the agreement and follow the bot's instructions. The acceptance is recorded against the contributor's GitHub account. No Git commit sign-off trailer or manually emailed form is required for the automated process. The paper signature fields in the Harmony template are not used for electronic acceptance in a pull request. Contributions owned by an employer or another organization cannot be accepted under this process. Contact the maintainer before submitting them.

The CLA process and legal terms require final review before contributions can be accepted under this policy. Existing contributions are not retroactively covered by a new CLA.

## Third-party material

Submit only material you own and are authorized to license under the CLA and project terms. If an employer or other party controls relevant rights, contact the maintainer before submitting.

Do not submit third-party source code, images, or other copyrighted material unless the maintainer has explicitly reviewed its origin, license, attribution requirements, and compatibility with *both* GPL distribution and any proposed proprietary licensing. Ordinary third-party dependencies must retain their respective licenses; the CLA cannot change them.

## Pull requests

Describe what the change does, include relevant tests, and note any new dependencies or third-party material. Review the [threat model](THREAT_MODEL.md) when changing security-sensitive behavior.
