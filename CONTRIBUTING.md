# Contributing to Sealbreak

Thank you for your interest in Sealbreak.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before merging a copyright-bearing contribution, contributors must accept the Harmony Contributor License Agreement (CLA) with **Outbound License Option Five**. Contributors keep copyright ownership, while granting the maintainer the additional rights needed for commercial and proprietary licensing.

Choose the appropriate document:

- [Individual Contributor License Agreement](docs/legal/CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md)
- [Entity Contributor License Agreement](docs/legal/CLA-ENTITY.md)

To accept the individual agreement, post this comment on your pull request from your GitHub account:

> I have read the Sealbreak Individual CLA 1.0 and I accept it.

The maintainer merges a pull request only after every person other than the maintainer who authored or co-authored one of its commits has posted this comment for the current agreement version. When the agreement receives a new version, returning contributors accept it again before their next pull request is merged. The comment stays in the public pull request history as the record of your acceptance. The paper signature fields in the Harmony template are not used. Company-owned contributions require separate authorization from an authorized representative under the Entity CLA.

Your acceptance covers all of your contributions to Sealbreak, including those you submitted before accepting (see "Effective Date" in the agreement). Contributions from people who have not accepted the agreement are not covered.

## Third-party material

Submit only material you own and are authorized to license under the CLA and project terms. If an employer or other party controls relevant rights, request approval or have that legal entity enter into the Entity CLA before merging.

If you used AI tools to create part of a contribution, say so in the pull request. You remain responsible for reviewing that output and for making sure it does not reproduce third-party material that you are not allowed to license.

Do not submit third-party source code, images, or other copyrighted material unless the maintainer has explicitly reviewed its origin, license, attribution requirements, and compatibility with *both* GPL distribution and any proposed proprietary licensing. Ordinary third-party dependencies must retain their respective licenses; the CLA cannot change them.

## Pull requests

Describe what the change does, include relevant tests, and note any new dependencies or third-party material. Review the [threat model](THREAT_MODEL.md) when changing security-sensitive behavior.
